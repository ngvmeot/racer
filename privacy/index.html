PRIVACY POLICY FOR NGV RACER

Last updated: 2026-11-10

INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy describes how we (Independent Developer) collect, use, and protect information in connection with the NGV Racer mobile application (the "Service" or "App") and our website meot.me. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT AND HOW IT IS USED

NGV Racer is a fully offline, local bicycle computer. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any external servers. All information remains strictly on your device.

1. User-Provided Technical Data (Local Storage Only)
To provide advanced cycling metrics, you may optionally enter the following technical data within the App settings:
- Resting Heart Rate and Maximum Heart Rate (used exclusively to calculate your heart rate zones).
- Weight (used exclusively to calculate your power-to-weight ratio, W/kg).
- Functional Threshold Power (FTP) (used exclusively to calculate your power zones).
This data is stored locally on your device, and we have no access to it.

2. Precise Location Data (GPS) and Cycling Metrics
To track your rides, calculate speed, distance, and generate route analytics, the App requires access to your device’s Precise Location Data (GPS). 
- While riding, the App displays real-time statistics, including speed, distance, and data from connected sensors (cadence, heart rate, power).
- If enabled, this data is compiled into a standardized .FIT file format.
- All .FIT files are saved strictly within the App's local directory on your device. You have full control over these files and can manually export, share, or delete them at any time.

3. Foreground Service and Activity Tracking
To ensure continuous and uninterrupted recording of your ride data when your phone's screen is turned off or when you use other apps, XYZ Racer utilizes an Android Foreground Service. 
- A persistent notification is always visible in your device's notification tray while the service is active.
- This notification clearly indicates to the user that the App is currently tracking a workout and prevents the operating system from terminating the process.
- We do not use Background Location tracking when the App is closed or not actively recording a ride via the Foreground Service.

4. Local Backup and Portability
The App includes a local backup feature, allowing you to export your settings, history, and bike profiles to a local file. This file is intended solely for data portability (e.g., transferring your data to a new device) and is managed entirely by you.

5. Bike Profiles and Sensor Logs
You can create profiles for your bicycles and scan for external Bluetooth/ANT+ cycling sensors. The configurations, saved sensor IDs, and ride history summaries are stored locally and are used only to manage your equipment.

DATA SHARING AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information or location data with any third parties. 

However, the App is distributed through the Google Play Store and operates on the Android platform. The Service relies on standard infrastructure services provided by Google, which may automatically collect technical data (such as device identifiers, operating system version, and crash logs) in accordance with their own privacy standards:

- Google Play Services (https://policies.google.com/privacy)

These system services are used exclusively to ensure the stable operation, licensing, and delivery of the App.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our Service is intended for a general audience and we do not knowingly collect or request personal information or location data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). 

Since NGV Racer operates entirely offline and stores all workout statistics and settings locally on the device, we do not maintain any user database or cloud profiles. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has stored data within the App, you can completely and immediately erase all such information at any time by clearing the App's storage/cache via the Android system settings or by uninstalling the App from the device.

DATA RETENTION

Since NGV Racer does not collect personal data or store information on external servers, we do not retain your data in any central database. All information—including your ride history, technical settings, and generated .FIT files—is stored exclusively on your device's local storage.

This data is retained indefinitely by your device for as long as the App remains installed, or until you choose to delete it manually (either by managing files within the App, clearing the App's data via Android settings, or uninstalling the App). We have no ability to recover or restore your data once it has been deleted from your device.

YOUR RIGHTS

Under data protection laws (such as GDPR and CCPA), users have specific rights regarding their information. Because NGV Racer operates strictly as a local offline application, you can exercise these rights independently at any time without contacting us:

• Right of Access and Portability: You can view all your ride history, statistics, and sensor data directly within the App. You can also export your data manually via standard .FIT files or the local backup feature to transfer it anywhere you choose.
• Right to Rectification: You can change, update, or correct your personal metrics (such as weight, FTP, or heart rate zones) at any moment in the App settings.
• Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can completely erase all your data from existence by clearing the App's data/cache through your Android system settings or by uninstalling the App from your device.

Since we do not hold your data on any server, we cannot modify, export, or delete it on your behalf.

DATA SECURITY

The security of your data is a top priority. Because NGV Racer operates entirely offline, the most effective security measure is built into its core design: your location logs, sensor data, and physical metrics are never transmitted over the internet to any external servers, significantly reducing the risk of unauthorized access or data breaches.

We rely on the standard operating system security features provided by Android to protect the data stored within the App's local directory on your device. However, please remember that the security of your information also depends on you. No method of electronic storage or device physical security is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We recommend securing your mobile device with a passcode or biometric lock to prevent unauthorized access to your local data.

INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Because NGV Racer operates strictly as a local, offline application and does not transmit any information to external servers or cloud storage, your data remains exclusively on your mobile device. 

Consequently, we do not transfer, store, or process your ride history, sensor logs, or physical metrics across international borders. All data processing occurs entirely within the country or region where you operate the App on your device.
